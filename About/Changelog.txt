0.1.0 - Initial grain-growing, processing and cooking release / 穀類の栽培・加工・料理の初回版
- Moves meal-tray serving and compatibility to the required AMJ Dining mod (currently using Harmony and Daily Menu). Agriculture retains food roles and retires new generic simple-meal Bills while preserving existing Def references.
- 一膳化・配膳・互換処理を必須ModのAMJ Diningへ移管（現在Harmony・Daily Menu使用）。料理の役割指定と汎用簡単な食事の新規Bill停止はAgricultureが維持する。

- Adds seven ancient/medieval Japanese staple grains, including a shared dry-field rice plant, with distinct growth and soil characteristics.
- Adds threshing and hulling, four active grain flours and a brown-rice/white-rice processing choice (paddy 10 -> brown 10 or polished white 8).
- Adds nine grain-based dishes with eighteen single/four-serving cooking Bills. Four specialty flour foods and the two white-rice dishes have distinct eating-mood rewards.
- Requires Medieval Overhaul as the food-production foundation, reusing existing MO wheat, flour, millstone and straw; historical Vanilla-only fallback source stays archived/dormant.
- Integrates the former Crop Growth Balance timings for six Vanilla and seven MO crops. Medieval Overhaul is required; Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul remains optional. No separate Crop Growth Balance Mod is needed.
- 日本の七穀（畑地の稲を含む）、脱穀・殻取り・4種の穀粉、籾から玄米／白米への加工を追加。
- 共通3料理・指定粉食4料理・白米2料理の合計9料理、単品と4食分の計18調理Billを実装。指定粉食・白米料理には食後の心情効果を設定。
- MOを必須とし、小麦・粉・石臼・藁などの既存工程を再利用する。旧Vanilla単独経路のソース・テスト履歴は保全。旧Crop Growth Balanceの生育日数調整を統合し、CCTOは任意。
