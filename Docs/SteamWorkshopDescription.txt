[b]An in-development Mod for dry-field grains, harvest processing and milling inspired by the everyday food supply of pre-Edo Japan.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Awa (foxtail millet), Hie (Japanese barnyard millet) and Kibi (proso millet), with different growing-season, temperature, fertility and harvest trade-offs
[*]Soba (buckwheat), barley, wheat and the shared rice plant for ordinary fields (no paddy mechanics)
[*]Threshing, hulling and four grain flours (wheat, buckwheat, millet and barley); existing Vanilla stoves also handle processing while dedicated equipment is being developed
[*]Gameplay-oriented differences between grains rather than interchangeable crop statistics
[/list]

This is a focused agriculture module, not an all-in-one AMJ Core. Rice paddies, fermentation and the broader Japanese settlement systems belong to other independent AMJ projects.

[h2]Food options and balance[/h2]
Three common grain/flour dishes can use leftover ingredients. Four flour-specific dishes provide a +2 mood bonus, while the separate brown/white rice meals and porridges include +3 for white rice meals and +1 for white rice porridge (each lasting 0.5 days). There are 11 normally producible dishes, each with one- and four-serving cooking Bills.

[h2]Q. What about rice flour?[/h2]
Rice flour existed in ancient and medieval Japan, but there is insufficient evidence that brown-rice flour was an everyday ingredient of medieval commoners. Separate rice-flour milling and stockpiling also add little gameplay value here. Rice-flour milling and dumpling cooking are excluded from normal production; brown and white rice meals and porridges remain. Historically supported ceremonial foods or confections may justify reconsidering rice flour later.

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]
[b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]. [b]No additional Mod is required.[/b] Medieval Overhaul (MO) is optional: with MO, Grains reuses its wheat, flour, millstone and straw integration; without MO, Grains provides its own wheat and milling path. Grains' own crop and processing rules take priority in both configurations. [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO)[/url] is also optional for crop-specific cold behavior.

[b]Strongly recommended (not required):[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400037215]Adaptive Primitive Storage[/url]. Its granary provides bulk storage for Grains' sheaves, kernels and flours. The original granary and baskets still use Hay. When MO is also installed, optional Straw-built versions appear separately in the construction menu. Choose either Hay or MO Straw; both are not required.

[h2]日本語 / Japanese[/h2]

[b]江戸以前の日本の食生活を参考に、畑作穀物の栽培・収穫後加工・製粉を扱う開発中のModです。[/b]

[h2]主な特徴[/h2]
[list]
[*]アワ・ヒエ・キビを追加し、生育期間・気温・肥沃度・収量の違いで作物を選べるようにする
[*]ソバ・大麦・小麦と、共通の「稲」を畑地で栽培する（水田機能は含まない）
[*]脱穀・殻取り・4種（小麦・蕎麦・雑穀・大麦）の穀粉への製粉ができる。暫定的に既存調理台も加工に利用する
[*]単純な上位互換ではなく、土地や季節に応じて穀物を植え分ける農業
[/list]

AMJ全体の共通基盤Modではなく、穀類とその基本加工を担う独立した構成要素です。水田稲作・発酵・集落生活のほかの仕組みは別のAMJ Modで扱います。

[h2]料理とバランス[/h2]
余った穀粒や4種の穀粉を使う共通の飯・粥・粉食に加え、材料指定の粉食4品、玄米・白米それぞれの飯・粥4品がある（合計11品、1食・4食の調理が可能）。指定粉食は食後心情+2、白飯は+3、白粥は+1（それぞれ0.5日）。

[h2]Q.米粉は？[/h2]
米粉自体は古代・中世日本にも存在した。しかし、玄米粉が中世庶民の日常食材だったとする十分な根拠は確認できない。独立した製粉・備蓄工程を増やすゲーム上の必要性も低いため、通常の米粉製粉と米粉団子の調理は対象外とした。玄米・白米の飯・粥は残る。将来、史料に基づく儀礼食・菓子を実装する場合に再検討する。

[h2]必須Mod・互換性[/h2]
[b]RimWorld 1.6対応。追加の必須Modはありません。[/b] Medieval Overhaul（MO）は任意です。MO併用時はMOの小麦・小麦粉・石臼・藁を連携利用し、MOなしではGrains側の小麦・製粉を利用します。いずれもGrains所有の作物・加工設定を優先します。[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul（CCTO）[/url]も任意です。

[b]併用を強く推奨（必須ではない）：[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400037215]Adaptive Primitive Storage[/url]。既存の穀倉でGrainsの穀物束・穀粒・穀粉を大量収納できる。元の穀倉・編み籠は干し草のまま。MO併用時は藁を使う別の建築項目も追加し、干し草版・藁版から選択できる。両方の素材を要求しない。
