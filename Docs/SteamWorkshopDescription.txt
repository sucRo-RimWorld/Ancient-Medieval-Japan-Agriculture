[h1]Ancient & Medieval Japan - Grains（中世日本 - 穀類）[/h1]

[b]An in-development Mod for dry-field grains, harvest processing and milling inspired by the everyday food supply of pre-Edo Japan.[/b]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Awa (foxtail millet), Hie (Japanese barnyard millet) and Kibi (proso millet), with different growing-season, temperature, fertility and harvest trade-offs
[*]Soba (buckwheat), barley, wheat and the shared rice plant for ordinary fields (no paddy mechanics)
[*]Threshing, hulling and five grain flours; existing Vanilla stoves also handle processing while dedicated equipment is being developed
[*]Gameplay-oriented differences between grains rather than interchangeable crop statistics
[/list]

This is a focused agriculture module, not an all-in-one AMJ Core. Rice paddies, fermentation and the broader Japanese settlement systems belong to other independent AMJ projects.

[h2]V1 food options and balance[/h2]
Mixed dishes can use leftover grain kernels or flours. Ingredient-specific flour dishes offer better preservation and a small mood bonus. See the bilingual food comparison image below.

[h2]Requirements and compatibility[/h2]
[b]RimWorld 1.6[/b]. [b]No additional Mod is required.[/b] Medieval Overhaul (MO) is optional: with MO, Grains reuses its wheat, flour, millstone and straw integration; without MO, Grains provides its own wheat and milling path. Grains' own crop and processing rules take priority in both configurations. [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO)[/url] is also optional for crop-specific cold behavior.

[b]Strongly recommended (not required):[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400037215]Adaptive Primitive Storage[/url]. Its granary provides bulk storage for Grains' sheaves, kernels and flours. With MO installed too, the granary and woven baskets use MO Straw instead of Hay as construction material.

[h2]Development status[/h2]
[b]Development build, not ready for public release.[/b] The hard MO requirement has been removed. A previous-source 1.7 four-profile native check passed; the current 1.6 subscriber build, translated UI, equipment variants, balance and final graphics are still unverified.

[h2]日本語 / Japanese[/h2]

[b]江戸以前の日本の食生活を参考に、畑作穀物の栽培・収穫後加工・製粉を扱う開発中のModです。[/b]

[h2]主な特徴[/h2]
[list]
[*]アワ・ヒエ・キビを追加し、生育期間・気温・肥沃度・収量の違いで作物を選べるようにする
[*]ソバ・大麦・小麦と、共通の「稲」を畑地で栽培する（水田機能は含まない）
[*]脱穀・殻取り・5種の穀粉への製粉ができる。暫定的に既存調理台も加工に利用する
[*]単純な上位互換ではなく、土地や季節に応じて穀物を植え分ける農業
[/list]

AMJ全体の共通基盤Modではなく、穀類とその基本加工を担う独立した構成要素です。水田稲作・発酵・集落生活のほかの仕組みは別のAMJ Modで扱います。

[h2]V1料理の性能比較[/h2]
余った穀粒・穀粉は混合料理に使える。材料指定の粉食5品は保存性と心情で有利。数値は末尾の英日併記画像に示す。

[h2]必須Mod・互換性[/h2]
[b]RimWorld 1.6対応。追加の必須Modはありません。[/b] Medieval Overhaul（MO）は任意です。MO併用時はMOの小麦・小麦粉・石臼・藁を連携利用し、MOなしではGrains側の小麦・製粉を利用します。いずれもGrains所有の作物・加工設定を優先します。[url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812412548]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul（CCTO）[/url]も任意です。

[b]併用を強く推奨（必須ではない）：[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3400037215]Adaptive Primitive Storage[/url]。既存の穀倉でGrainsの穀物束・穀粒・穀粉を大量収納できる。MOも導入している場合は、穀倉・編み籠の建築材料を干し草からMOの藁へ切り替える。

[h2]開発状況[/h2]
[b]開発ビルドであり、公開準備は完了していません。[/b] MO必須指定は解除しました。旧ソースのRimWorld 1.7四構成実機検証には合格記録がありますが、現行1.6の配布版・日英UI・設備別の実動作・バランス・専用画像は未確認です。
