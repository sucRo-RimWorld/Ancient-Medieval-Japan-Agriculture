# Five-flour completion: prior-art and compatibility entry audit
Date: 2026-10-13 JST. Owner: sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Grains. Scope: complete the already approved separate barley and rice flours and shared milling, preserving existing grain processing and optional MO. Finished food labels, historical descriptions and release approval are separate editorial gates.

## Evidence inspected and comparison bounds
- Vanilla 1.6: Existing RawRice and meal infrastructure; the AMJ-owned rice sheaf -> hull -> RawRice chain is in Docs/Balance/Crops/RicePostHarvestProcessing.md. We do not replace RawRice or change Vanilla food definitions. No full external Vanilla binary/source was provided to this review.
- Medieval Overhaul 1.6: supplied 3219596926.zip inspected earlier in AMJ project; DankPyon_Flour and DankPyon_Millstone with native wheat-flour Bills exist. Existing Grains code reuses the millstone conditionally and prevents duplicate MO wheat flour. Barley/rice must remain independent named flours, not generic DankPyon_Flour. Archive data may differ from future MO updates.
- Existing Grains: Docs/Design.md section 5, Defs/RecipeDefs/Recipes_GrainsMilling.xml, Defs/ThingDefs_Items/Items_GrainsFlour.xml, Compatibility/MedievalOverhaul/Patches/MedievalOverhaul_GrainsFlour.xml implement millet, buckwheat, and wheat. Missing barley/rice is an approved feature gap, not justification to redefine those items.
- VE: Vanilla Cooking Expanded, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2134308519 (Workshop 1.6 listing checked 2026-10-13), provides a generic flour and broader cooking systems and depends on Vanilla Expanded Framework. It does not establish a drop-in AMJ-owned 5-flour postharvest system. Source XML not obtained; no claim of proven absence of any specific rice/barley Recipe.
- non-VE: Grains and Brewing, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2897396824 (Workshop listing checked 2026-10-13), offers barley and grain interactions and VCE-linked milling; listed version 1.4+/1.5, so suitability for AMJ 1.6 is unverified. It is a broader agriculture, brewing and pickling dependency, not a narrow substitute.
- non-VE: AF Dumpling, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3139924720 (Workshop listing checked 2026-10-13), provides filled dumplings/raw dough and special stoves. Its scope differs from the small AMJ flour-to-food loop; source XML not obtained.
- Additional broader agriculture compatibility reference: Progression Agriculture, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3418017359 , documents explicit MO/VCE flour de-duplication, supporting a no-double-flour strategy.

## Decision
Continue Grains-owned barley flour and rice flour as separate named resources. Rice flour deliberately combines non-glutinous and glutinous rice FLOUR conceptually while RawRice remains the only game input. Keep 10 edible grain -> 10 flour, without increasing total nutrition, preserve existing milling equipment and MO conditional reuse. No VE or non-VE dependency is justified for these two small approved resource extensions. No third-party asset or code is copied.
Do not misidentify unroasted barley flour as historical roasted hattai. Japanese historical food prose must receive author approval before being translated/published; a pounded glutinous-rice mochi is distinct from flour-based dumplings. Select finished food Recipes only after verifying the historically correct processing steps and duplicate use against current Grains food.

## Limits and acceptance
This is a bounded search against listed Workshop descriptions and existing/local MO source, NOT exhaustive or full third-party XML audit. Confirm loaded RawRice nutrition and all four real-provider profiles in RimWorld; verify StackCount graphics and real Bill jobs before calling implementation complete.
