## History and certainty

- Yao Keisuke, ALIC, *History of sugar food in Japan*: https://www.alic.go.jp/joho-s/joho07_000217.html. Mentions `さたうまんぢう` in `七十一番職人歌合` and sugar yokan in `庭訓往来`, supporting sugar confection names in Muromachi-era records; **not a verified sugar-manju ingredient formula** or mood, work, price, nutrition or image. Prior invented prototype Draft PR #264 was closed unmerged; no asset/recipe adopted.
- National Diet Library, collaborative reference answer on Pancake and Hotcake: https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/reference/show?id=1000354094&page=ref_view. Modern Japanese pancake introduction is Meiji (1884 named printed reference) and common commercial hotcake is Taisho (1923). This does not rule out all historic skillet bread worldwide, only MO's **specific syrup-stacked Western confection** as routine Japanese medieval food.
- Harada Nobuo, *Edo Fruit: Grapes and Wine*, National Museum of Japanese History botanical bulletin No.176 (2007): https://archive.rekihaku.ac.jp/exhibitions/plant/column/2007/176.pdf. Publicly indexed abstract/text indicates no reliable Japanese grape cultivation attestation through Muromachi and 1186 origin has weak support. **Limit:** original PDF open produced 502; visual page review unavailable, no more precise claim made.
- Yamanashi Prefecture historic grapes: https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/oishii-mirai/nochikusanbutsu/budou.html ; Kofu city: https://www.city.kofu.yamanashi.jp/welcome/brand/budo.html . 718/1186 early-vine origins presented explicitly as legends; `本朝食鑑` (1695) corroborates Edo specialty, not medieval documentation.
- Agency for Cultural Affairs preserved grape/vine tools: https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/311/00000248 . Describes Edo trellis expansion and Meiji modern winemaking.

**Decision boundary:** 718/1186 traditions are not proof of cultivated Japanese grapes in Kamakura/Muromachi; absence of contemporary records is not proof no grapevine ever existed. MO `DankPyon_Plant_Grape` plus `DankPyon_VinePost`/raw fruit are source-retained, pending explicit historic crop adoption, with no current grape numeric, sow tag, images or research change. Existing `MO_FruitWineProductionExclusion.xml` preserves imported finished wine while disconnecting local modern grape/mulberry wine; ancient mulberry sericulture is unaffected. Finished `DankPyon_Sugar` stays trader count3–6 and value5.0. No replacement meal is forced even when no approved cooking Bill consumes it.

**Validation boundary:** MO-original XML XPath targeted match and XML syntax are static checks, NOT loaded 1.6/1.7 kitchen, trade, old-save or economy acceptance; native runs deliberately not triggered.

## 2026-10-25 added evidence: revised grape chronology and carrot name distinction

This section **supersedes** the earlier simplified blanket statement about no Muromachi evidence; the older historical summary is retained for provenance, not as an independently correct current conclusion. Source-level agricultural implementations remain unchanged.

### Grape: contemporary and later material, with certainty tiers

1. **Contemporary 1491 verse attached to an extant painting:** 没倫紹等『葡萄図』, 延徳3年（1491）. 東京国立博物館's curator reports that the artist's inscription mentions ten autumn days of storm, a grape trellis not falling, and numerous grapes on the vine. The curator suggests an intimate, near-at-hand scene. https://www.tnm.jp/modules/rblog/1/2013/06/21/%E8%91%A1%E8%90%84%E5%9B%B3/ and Agency for Cultural Affairs https://online.bunka.go.jp/heritages/detail/539345 . **Meaning:** real dated Muromachi textual evidence of a fruiting cultivated-style trellis being known to a Japanese monk; **limits:** inscription does not name a farm, owner, prefecture, planted cultivar, output or whether the verse describes first-hand Japanese agriculture versus a literary motif. Do not infer widespread farming or a certain first-cultivation year.
2. **Merchant eyewitness tradition at the historical boundary:** アビラ・ヒロン（1594年に平戸到着、1619年まで断続的に日本に滞在）は『日本王国記』で長崎周辺の白・黒二種類の葡萄に言及。 https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%81%82%E3%81%B3%E3%82%89%E3%81%B2%E3%82%8D%E3%82%93-3231126 . Scholar 原田信男「江戸のブドウとブドウ酒」『酒史研究』24（2008） pp.1–10 uses this as evidence of limited local late-Sengoku/early-Edo cultivation, particularly white grapes; document indexed at https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1520853833928500480 and https://shusigakkai1.web.fc2.com/kakoron/24/24_01-10.pdf . **Limit:** original scholar PDF unavailable for full visual inspection (web 503); only indexed excerpts compared. Nor does Giron provide a nationally representative census, crop-cycle data, or a verifiable pre-1600 harvest date.
3. **Historically untrusted origin stories:** 718 行基 and 1186 雨宮 cultivated-grape origin are labelled legends by 山梨県 https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/oishii-mirai/nochikusanbutsu/budou.html . 山梨県立図書館年表 https://www.lib.pref.yamanashi.jp/kosyu/manabu/nenpyo_1.html lists a 1549 武田家臣葡萄献上感状, but scholar 原田 (2008) judges the claimed original to be a *forged* document on documentary form/phrasing; **do not use that dated letter as verified evidence**. The same library marks 1594 浅野 fruit-promotion as an **origin theory**, not a contemporaneous crop register. 1695『本朝食鑑』甲州ブドウ産地 first-ranking description *is* later Edo evidence, not medieval proof.
4. **京都大宮葡萄:** 京都府立図書館 2017 reference https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/reference/show?id=1000222764 says a later 1984 Kyoto encyclopedia considers cultivation **possibly begun in late medieval times**; the cited 『毛吹草』『雍州府志』 are later Edo documentation. This is **secondary inference** for late-Muromachi date, not a new contemporary agricultural ledger.

**Result:** in-game cultivated grapes may be a defensible *restricted late-Muromachi/Sengoku* possibility; previous categorical absence-of-record assertions were too strong. However deciding a **generally available crop** (vs occasional local cultivation, collection, import, or an era-restricted source) and its actual MO cultivar/plant art/yield/trellis/research remains **HOLD**. Do not change `DankPyon_Plant_Grape`, `DankPyon_VinePost`, fruit or wine imports/exclusions merely on this research.

### Eastern carrot: dated attestation and homonym trap

- 農林水産省 https://www.maff.go.jp/j/kids/agri/roots/region03.html : Eastern `Daucus carota` types from China in **early Edo**; later Western carrots distinct.
- 農畜産業振興機構 https://vegetable.alic.go.jp/yasaijoho/yasai/0502_yasai1.html : **late 16th to 17th century** arrival for Eastern-type carrots, with long slender roots and several colors. This range does *not* prove early general cultivation.
- 『精選版日本国語大辞典』 `人参`/`胡蘿蔔` https://kotobank.jp/word/%E4%BA%BA%E5%8F%82-593771 : 1631『多識編』 entry identifies the edible vegetable as `胡蘿蔔`/`せりにんじん`, a concrete *Japanese written attestation*. Early `続日本紀` 739 `人参三十斤` is classified as medicinal **朝鮮人参**, not edible carrot, so no ancient Daucus-carota evidence can be inferred from that homonym. The 1631 mention itself cannot date the arrival precisely or prove Muromachi cultivation.
- NDL literature survey https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/reference/show?id=1000307854 : cites secondary dictionaries/food histories offering 16th-century/late-Sengoku introduction estimates. They are *reported estimates*, not new observed pre-1600 harvest documents.

**Result:** the Eastern carrot botanical identity is plausible only near the late Sengoku–early Edo border; historically routine ancient-to-mid-medieval planting is **not supported**. Retain existing AMJ+MO plant/food/recipe, cultivar representation, growDays6, yield6, skill4, research, trade and loot **unchanged** until a deliberate era/crop admission decision and dependency comparison. The current `IntermediateAgriculture` research level is **not a historical calendar-year lock**. Modern orange Western-root graphics remain provisional; earlier carrot cake was already disabled. No new substitute crop, additional cooking, or arbitrary crop exclusion is approved.
