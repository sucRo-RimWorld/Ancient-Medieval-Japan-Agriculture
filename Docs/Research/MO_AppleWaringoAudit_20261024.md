## 1. Period and species: why Western orchard apples are the wrong model

- NARO, ["りんごのお話"](https://www.naro.go.jp/publicity_report/season/006523.html): small and sour **waringo** reached Japan from China **during/after the Heian period**, but today's large Western apple is a different kind introduced in the Meiji period.
- Japanese Encyclopedia / [Kotobank, リンゴ](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%82%8A%E3%82%93%E3%81%94-758082): the fruit is documented as `林檎` from around the mid-Kamakura period; **the cultivation spread mainly in the Edo period**, whereas Western apples were introduced in the 19th century. There is uncertainty over the actual Heian arrival and how widespread cultivation was in earlier centuries; do not imply a common early-medieval staple orchard.
- [Ringo University, fruit history](https://www.ringodaigaku.com/play/ichiki/index_5.html): late-medieval `林檎` are documented as **gifts/offering fruits** especially for higher-status people rather than a mass high-calorie crop. Describes *about 3.5–4 cm in diameter*, red-tinged, with mild acidity and sweetness, ripening around **late July–August** and **poor storage** (these are historical/heritage cultivar characteristics, not precise yield measurements).
- [GKZ plant encyclopedia, waringo](https://gkzplant.sakura.ne.jp/mokuhon/syousai/wagyou/warinngo.html): `Malus asiatica` (traditional identification), China-origin small flattened-round fruit, ~3–4 cm, red/pink-to-yellow ripening color. Taxonomic identifications and individual traditional cultivars may differ; do not imply every pre-Edo apple was identical.

**Interpretation:** The appropriately modeled player resource is a **rare/specialty, seasonal small fruit** (not the primary diet). **Proposed design direction:** reuse the already-present MO tree/fruit identity as the AMJ waringo carrier rather than add a competing tree/item or indiscriminately exclude historic apples. Reinterpretation still requires real physical changes before marking completed.

**Verified engine distinction:** `harvestAfterGrowth=0.30` is the **post-harvest retained-tree growth fraction** when `harvestAfterGrowth>0` (`Plant.PlantCollected` and `PlantProperties.HarvestDestroys=false`); it also affects the graphical stage. It is NOT the point at which harvesting becomes available. With no `harvestMinGrowth` override, `PlantProperties` defaults to **0.65**. `Plant.HarvestableNow` tests `Growth>0.65`, and `Plant.YieldNow` gives ~**50%** of the base `harvestYield` immediately above 0.65, increasing to 100% only at growth 1.0 (before HP/difficulty/pawn harvest factors). Thus the original **20** apples is the full-growth yield, not necessarily the amount obtained each auto-harvest. The exact original MO 1.6 `TreeBase` loaded inheritance and ordinary pawn harvest decisions remain an empirical native verification gate; underlying code cross-check used a separate RimWorld decompilation, not the target compiled 1.6 binary. Source: [RimWorld Plant Rendering](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Modding_Tutorials/Plant_Rendering) and [RimWorld Crop growth](https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Crop).

4. **Crop numerics:** **No numerical change yet**. The smaller physical fruit does not alone give a lawful `harvestYield=20→X` conversion because item count is fruit units rather than kilograms and Nutrition0.05 is a game abstraction. Evaluate `harvestYield`, `Nutrition`, `growDays`, `harvestAfterGrowth`, `daysToRotStart`, sow skill, high/low-fertility, and season/latitude together, not a single attractive guess. Original `growDays=33`, `harvestYield=20`, `Nutrition=0.05` imply **1.0 raw Nutrition per full nominal base 20-item harvest** before skill/age/condition modifiers, but the number is a game arithmetic fact, *not evidence about real waringo weight or annual productivity*.

MO Japanization must inspect the full research graph before reassigning Apple **and Mulberry** together, preserving `DankPyon_Silkworm`. 6. **Localization:** Replace/remove Western-apple description **in both EN and JA** only as part of the coordinated visual/production source. MO Japanese translator has its own 1.6 DefInjected description; check effective precedence. Do not assert successful in-game translation from source inspection alone.
7. **Engine/compatibility gates:** Change only affected MO-conditional fields, preserve identifiers, load in original 1.6 with/without MO and no Ideology, verify ordinary plant menu, tree mature/immature depiction, genuine harvest, raw fruit/cold storage tooltip, save reload, no resurfacing pastry/cider, and effective Japanese text. ## 3a. Independent bounded growth/harvest check — 2026-10-24

**Source and mechanism:** The original MO 1.6.2.2 Apple Tree has `growDays=33`, `fertilitySensitivity=0.70`, `harvestAfterGrowth=0.30`, `harvestYield=20`, `harvestedThingDef=DankPyon_RawApples`, `Nutrition=0.05` per apple and no explicit `harvestMinGrowth`. Separate source reading of [PlantProperties](https://github.com/Dyyrlysh/RimworldDecompile/blob/79ca2aa15c9e116bd43d448692b62636467f6c46/RimWorld/PlantProperties.cs) and [Plant](https://github.com/Dyyrlysh/RimworldDecompile/blob/79ca2aa15c9e116bd43d448692b62636467f6c46/RimWorld/Plant.cs) shows default harvest minimum **0.65**, post-harvest **reset to 0.30** (non-destructive), initial newly sown growth ~0, and a **50% normal base harvest** just above 0.65. `YieldNow` does not itself apply the pawn plant-harvest-yield stat; the job/harvesting pipeline can also adjust real output. Graphic stage transition is a distinct concern: mature art may show fruit at growth ≳0.30, before the plant is harvestable.

**Not a native test. Declared simplifications**: temperature/light growth multipliers 1.0 whenever awake; plant grows **13 of 24 game hours** (rest 11h); no shade, rain, snow, frost, blight, sowing or harvest job time, harvest failure, animal grazing, winter retention/damage, age-based lifespan, processing or planting restrictions. `fertility` remains a static percent multiplier `F = (1 - 0.7) + 0.7 × fertility`, with no manually imposed 200% cap. A full-strength time to first harvest and next cycle are:
- `firstCalendarDays = 33 × 0.65 ÷ ((13/24) × F)`
- `repeatCalendarDays = 33 × (0.65 - 0.30) ÷ ((13/24) × F)`
- `expectedSingleHarvest ≈ 20 × 0.5 = 10` items at earliest allowable growth (before difficulty/skill/HP), **0.5 raw Nutrition** rather than `20×0.05=1.0` immediately.
- The default `TreeBase` inherited lifespan and actual seasons, along with any CCTO temperature response, can change the real observed multiple-year result; these are not part of the model.

| Static soil fertility | F | First harvest, calendar days | Time per repeat, calendar days | At 30-day ideal growing window | At 60-day fully ideal year |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 70% | 0.79 | 50.1 | 27.0 | 0 harvest, 0 apple | 1 harvest, ~10 apples |
| 100% | 1.00 | 39.6 | 21.3 | 0 harvest, 0 apple | 1 harvest, ~10 apples |
| 140% | 1.28 | 30.9 | 16.7 | 0 harvest, 0 apple | 2 harvests, ~20 apples |
| 200% (comparison only) | 1.70 | 23.3 | 12.5 | 1 harvest, ~10 apples | 3 harvests, ~30 apples |

Rows assume player harvests as soon as available; allowing fruit to reach growth=1.0 increases yield per harvest but delays subsequent cycles. Results are discrete and simplified, not promises for generated AMJ biome maps. At 100% soil and 30 days remaining the crop produces **no apples despite surviving as a tree**. On a 60-day year with 100% soil the tree nominally provides **0.5 raw Nutrition**, versus a much higher food supply from sow-and-replant crops. For instance, at 100% soil, 60 constant ideal calendar days afford **32.5 effective grow-days**; for Agriculture's 5 effective-day / 11-paddy rice plant this is up to **6 completed harvest cycles** (~66 unprocessed paddy before processing), if temperature/light requirements, jobs and sowing actually permit. A 1:1 comparison of the two items as calories is **NOT** justified without the thresh/hull and work chain; the contrast does establish that original MO apple is not obviously an excessively productive staple in this bounded no-work model.

**Storage/economy cross-check, no new price:** Original MO apples and mulberries both have **15-day rot, 1.1 MarketValue, 0.05 raw Nutrition**; grapes have 14-day rot and 1.2 MarketValue. Those original values make apples not uniquely long-storing or priced as a luxury. The historic rarity/gift interpretation cannot automatically be represented by increasing raw price without examining trade stock and cultivation access; the `Cocoa` research requirement currently gates access in an historically incoherent way. Prior decision to disconnect Western pie and cider Bills also keeps apples from functioning as a high-value processed food chain.

**Decision at this stage:** **DO NOT retune `growDays=33`, `harvestYield=20`, `Nutrition=0.05`, `daysToRotStart=15` or `MarketValue=1.1` on the basis of appearance or raw base yield alone.** Existing values are not an accepted historical calibration, but the independent bounds show no already-proven need for emergency yield suppression. First resolve MO Japanization's research/graphics/tooltip adaptation, inspect ordinary actual harvest and short-season risk in one consolidated 1.6 check, then assess whether an actual balance contradiction remains. Do not quietly change all five parameters and erase causal visibility.
