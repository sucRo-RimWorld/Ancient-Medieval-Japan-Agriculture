## 1. Period and species: why Western orchard apples are the wrong model

- NARO, ["りんごのお話"](https://www.naro.go.jp/publicity_report/season/006523.html): small and sour **waringo** reached Japan from China **during/after the Heian period**, but today's large Western apple is a different kind introduced in the Meiji period.
- Japanese Encyclopedia / [Kotobank, リンゴ](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%82%8A%E3%82%93%E3%81%94-758082): the fruit is documented as `林檎` from around the mid-Kamakura period; **the cultivation spread mainly in the Edo period**, whereas Western apples were introduced in the 19th century. There is uncertainty over the actual Heian arrival and how widespread cultivation was in earlier centuries; do not imply a common early-medieval staple orchard.
- [Ringo University, fruit history](https://www.ringodaigaku.com/play/ichiki/index_5.html): late-medieval `林檎` are documented as **gifts/offering fruits** especially for higher-status people rather than a mass high-calorie crop. Describes *about 3.5–4 cm in diameter*, red-tinged, with mild acidity and sweetness, ripening around **late July–August** and **poor storage** (these are historical/heritage cultivar characteristics, not precise yield measurements).
- [GKZ plant encyclopedia, waringo](https://gkzplant.sakura.ne.jp/mokuhon/syousai/wagyou/warinngo.html): `Malus asiatica` (traditional identification), China-origin small flattened-round fruit, ~3–4 cm, red/pink-to-yellow ripening color. Taxonomic identifications and individual traditional cultivars may differ; do not imply every pre-Edo apple was identical.

**Interpretation:** The appropriately modeled player resource is a **rare/specialty, seasonal small fruit** (not the primary diet). **Proposed design direction:** reuse the already-present MO tree/fruit identity as the AMJ waringo carrier rather than add a competing tree/item or indiscriminately exclude historic apples. Reinterpretation still requires real physical changes before marking completed.

2. **Harvestable resource:** Retain eating raw apples and generic category links; no pies/cider resurrected. Treat this as a **minor supplementary fruit**/occasional gift, not high-yield staple farming. Keep original loot/trade routes pending independent rarity audit; do not invent forced imports or spontaneous wild trees.
3. **Visual minimum — implement through MO Japanization, NOT an Agriculture duplicate:** Retain original 128px MO sprite bounds and shared AMJ silhouette/outline style. Japanization can prepare candidates for the **mature tree** with much smaller green/yellow/blush fruit (not four large Western red apples) and **raw icon** with flattened small fruit and visually comparable carrier. Audit the immature tree and retain if consistent. Production assets are subject to Project art style, source preservation, transparency/shape/size checks and **explicit visual acceptance** before any source replacement. Do not publish a second Agriculture override for the same `DankPyon_` MO paths.
4. **Crop numerics:** **No numerical change yet**. The smaller physical fruit does not alone give a lawful `harvestYield=20→X` conversion because item count is fruit units rather than kilograms and Nutrition0.05 is a game abstraction. Evaluate `harvestYield`, `Nutrition`, `growDays`, `harvestAfterGrowth`, `daysToRotStart`, sow skill, high/low-fertility, and season/latitude together, not a single attractive guess. Original `growDays=33`, `harvestYield=20`, `Nutrition=0.05` imply **1.0 raw Nutrition per full nominal base 20-item harvest** before skill/age/condition modifiers, but the number is a game arithmetic fact, *not evidence about real waringo weight or annual productivity*.
5. **Research ownership:** Existing `Cocoa` is unacceptable as the *historical explanation*. MO Japanization must inspect the full research graph before reassigning Apple **and Mulberry** together, preserving `DankPyon_Silkworm`. Reusing an existing Agriculture/MO research project (e.g. a general crop or orchard unlock) is worth exploring, but **no specific DefName/tech tier is approved by this document**.
6. **Localization:** Replace/remove Western-apple description **in both EN and JA** only as part of the coordinated visual/production source. MO Japanese translator has its own 1.6 DefInjected description; check effective precedence. Do not assert successful in-game translation from source inspection alone.
7. **Engine/compatibility gates:** Change only affected MO-conditional fields, preserve identifiers, load in original 1.6 with/without MO and no Ideology, verify ordinary plant menu, tree mature/immature depiction, genuine harvest, raw fruit/cold storage tooltip, save reload, no resurfacing pastry/cider, and effective Japanese text. Other accepted MO crop XML remains intact. **Do not auto-run** a new native matrix merely to evaluate proposed numbers.

## 3a. Native 1.6 correction and independently bounded annual yield — 2026-10-24

**Test and provenance:** Original licensed Linux RimWorld **1.6.4871 rev598** on isolated Project native slot, source-pinned real MO+VEF+SYR plus six mandatory AMJ owners, including Japanization. The successful [Actions #40085164460](https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Project/actions/runs/40085164460) artifact `moj-waringo16-40085164460` (ID 12556523746, SHA256 `8c77fb8b27d6a35c51d6fc6daf14c05bedac5a03cfee11f2aa7a623bae76db32`) records 1 Pickle scenario PASS, Unity ERROR0, exit0, no timeout and source game unmodified. Japanese labels `和リンゴの木` / `和リンゴ`, fruit-research `果樹栽培`, Apple/Mulberry Cocoa research links and the tree harvest/reset engine path all passed. Loaded `harvestMinGrowth=0.40`, `harvestAfterGrowth=0.30`, `cropYieldFactor=1.2`; Growth0.66 YieldNow=17, full Growth1.00 YieldNow=24. The initial [Actions #40084606772](https://github.com/sucRo-RimWorld/Ancient-Medieval-Japan-Project/actions/runs/40084606772) ran the same full suite with Unity ERROR0 but Pickle0/1 because its *test premise* incorrectly expected base unadjusted 9–11 fruit at 0.66. Only that single scenario was rerun after correcting the assertion to use actual loaded harvest threshold/difficulty; do not erase the prior failed record or misattribute it to production code.

**Corrected effective-growth bounds:** Original `growDays=33`, `fertilitySensitivity=0.7`, `harvestYield=20`, `harvestAfterGrowth=.30` stay unchanged. Loaded `harvestMinGrowth=.40`, **not .65**. At **earliest** harvest after .40, per-harvest base yield tends to ~10 units at crop factor1.0 or ~12 at the observed factor1.2, before pawn harvest stats, rounding, HP loss etc. The original grow-day and fertility idealization is still only a bounded mathematical model:
- Ideal calendar hours with 13h/day of growth and no temperatures/season/damage/work delays.
- Fertility speed factor `F = 0.30 + 0.70 × soil_fertility` (soil_fertility as numeric proportion).
- `firstCalendarDays ≈ 33 × .40 / ((13/24) × F)`, `repeatCalendarDays ≈ 33 × (.40−.30) / ((13/24) × F)`.
- **First harvest and return** use a strictly greater-than 0.40 threshold in engine, so entries near the cutoff may shift by ticks; counts below are approximate and do not prove actual colony harvest efficiency.

| Soil fertility | Effective F | First harvest (ideal days) | Re-harvest (ideal days) | 30-day window (base yield ~10 each) | 60-day fully ideal window (base yield ~10 each) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 70% | 0.79 | 30.9 | 7.7 | 0 harvest | 4 harvests, ~40 fruit |
| 100% | 1.00 | 24.4 | 6.1 | 1 harvest, ~10 fruit | **6 harvests, ~60 fruit** |
| 140% | 1.28 | 19.0 | 4.8 | 3 harvests, ~30 fruit | **9 harvests, ~90 fruit** |
| 200% (comparison only) | 1.70 | 14.3 | 3.6 | 5 harvests, ~50 fruit | 13 harvests, ~130 fruit |

At the *tested* storyteller cropYieldFactor1.2, multiply the illustrative earliest-harvest fruit totals by approximately 1.2 (e.g. ~72 on 100% fertility across 60 fully ideal days, ~3.6 raw Nutrition using `Nutrition=0.05` per fruit). These are illustrative bounds, **not native 60-day runs**, and presuppose that a planter harvests almost immediately every cycle. Freezing weather, poor light, actual crop age/winter survival, inability to get pawns to auto-harvest, disease, failure, labor/work speed and alternate plant-yield multipliers may materially reduce output. No real ancient/medieval Japan apple yield is inferred from MO plant mechanics.

**Impact / decision gate:** Earlier §3a tables based on loaded 0.65 and statements that 100% fertility yields only ~10 apples in a 60-day year are **WITHDRAWN**. Actual 0.40 threshold plus short .30→.40 repeat window presents a **concrete risk of implausibly frequent waringo harvest**. Imported sugar and domestic orchard product balancing must not rely on the withdrawn assumptions. A simple bounded **unapproved candidate** is to set Apple-only `plant/harvestMinGrowth` explicitly to **0.65** (vanilla engine default), retaining `harvestAfterGrowth=.30`, `growDays=33` and base `harvestYield=20`, which mathematically predicts ~39.6 first / ~21.3 repeat calendar days on ideal 100% soil and only one earliest harvest within 60 ideal days. This would directly address repeated fruit harvesting without a new bloating annual-simulation framework; **it is NOT an author-accepted production value**. Evaluate both actual gameplay frequency and short-season usefulness, distinct from source/coverage, before changing production XML. Do not blindly adjust growth, calorie density, decay, market price and harvest threshold all at once. Keep images skipped by explicit user instruction.
