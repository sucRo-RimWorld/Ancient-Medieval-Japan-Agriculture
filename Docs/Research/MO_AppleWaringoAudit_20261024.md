## 1. Period and species: why Western orchard apples are the wrong model

- NARO, ["りんごのお話"](https://www.naro.go.jp/publicity_report/season/006523.html): small and sour **waringo** reached Japan from China **during/after the Heian period**, but today's large Western apple is a different kind introduced in the Meiji period.
- NARO's [130th-anniversary publication](https://www.naro.go.jp/publicity_report/publication/files/naro130th_anniversary_magazine.pdf) dates the introduction of waringo no later than the mid-Kamakura period and discusses its historical use as offerings. This is context, *not* apple orchard productivity data.
- Japanese Encyclopedia / [Kotobank, リンゴ](https://kotobank.jp/word/%E3%82%8A%E3%82%93%E3%81%94-758082): the fruit is documented as `林檎` from around the mid-Kamakura period; **the cultivation spread mainly in the Edo period**, whereas Western apples were introduced in the 19th century. There is uncertainty over the actual Heian arrival and how widespread cultivation was in earlier centuries; do not imply a common early-medieval staple orchard.
- [Ringo University, fruit history](https://www.ringodaigaku.com/play/ichiki/index_5.html): late-medieval `林檎` are documented as **gifts/offering fruits** especially for higher-status people rather than a mass high-calorie crop. Describes *about 3.5–4 cm in diameter*, red-tinged, with mild acidity and sweetness, ripening around **late July–August** and **poor storage** (these are historical/heritage cultivar characteristics, not precise yield measurements).
- [GKZ plant encyclopedia, waringo](https://gkzplant.sakura.ne.jp/mokuhon/syousai/wagyou/warinngo.html): `Malus asiatica` (traditional identification), China-origin small flattened-round fruit, ~3–4 cm, red/pink-to-yellow ripening color. Taxonomic identifications and individual traditional cultivars may differ; do not imply every pre-Edo apple was identical.

**Interpretation:** The appropriately modeled player resource is a **rare/specialty, seasonal small fruit** (not the primary diet). **Proposed design direction:** reuse the already-present MO tree/fruit identity as the AMJ waringo carrier rather than add a competing tree/item or indiscriminately exclude historic apples. Reinterpretation still requires real physical changes before marking completed.

## 3. Proposed unified redesign: minimum coherent slice

1. **Identity/compatibility:** Keep `DankPyon_Tree_Apple` and `DankPyon_RawApples`, tree/fruit category and original save references. Do **not** duplicate `Malus` tree. Game-facing JP candidate `リンゴ（和リンゴ）` / English `waringo (Japanese apple)` in descriptive material, with actual period document wording `林檎` when historical localization is chosen; final label is **subject to integrated art/text acceptance**, not approved here.
2. **Harvestable resource:** Retain eating raw apples and generic category links; no pies/cider resurrected. Treat this as a **minor supplementary fruit**/occasional gift, not high-yield staple farming. Keep original loot/trade routes pending independent rarity audit; do not invent forced imports or spontaneous wild trees.
3. **Visual minimum:** Retain the 128px sprite bounds and AMJ silhouette/outline style. Redesign **mature tree** to show *much smaller* mixed green/yellow/blush fruit, not 4 large modern Western apples; redesign the **raw fruit icon** as small flattened/subtly red-tinged fruits in an appropriate carrier. Audit the **immature tree** and reuse it if visually consistent; avoid 3-image remake without cause. Production art requires the actual style/transparent-PNG/source-preservation rules and **visual author acceptance** before replacing existing image bytes. Do not use unapproved image generation/crops or overwrite original accepted files.
4. **Crop numerics:** **No numerical change yet**. The smaller physical fruit does not alone give a lawful `harvestYield=20→X` conversion because item count is fruit units rather than kilograms and Nutrition0.05 is a game abstraction. Evaluate `harvestYield`, `Nutrition`, `growDays`, `harvestAfterGrowth`, `daysToRotStart`, sow skill, high/low-fertility, and season/latitude together, not a single attractive guess. Original `growDays=33`, `harvestYield=20`, `Nutrition=0.05` imply **1.0 raw Nutrition per full nominal base 20-item harvest** before skill/age/condition modifiers, but the number is a game arithmetic fact, *not evidence about real waringo weight or annual productivity*.
5. **Research ownership:** Existing `Cocoa` is unacceptable as the *historical explanation*. Coordinate MO Japanization research-graph replacement for Apple **and Mulberry** in one coherent silk/fruit access audit, preserving `DankPyon_Silkworm`. Reusing an existing Agriculture/MO research project (e.g. a general crop or orchard unlock) is worth exploring, but **no specific DefName/tech tier is approved by this document**.
6. **Localization:** Replace/remove Western-apple description **in both EN and JA** only as part of the coordinated visual/production source. MO Japanese translator has its own 1.6 DefInjected description; check effective precedence. Do not assert successful in-game translation from source inspection alone.
7. **Engine/compatibility gates:** Change only affected MO-conditional fields, preserve identifiers, load in original 1.6 with/without MO and no Ideology, verify ordinary plant menu, tree mature/immature depiction, genuine harvest, raw fruit/cold storage tooltip, save reload, no resurfacing pastry/cider, and effective Japanese text. Other accepted MO crop XML remains intact. **Do not auto-run** a new native matrix merely to evaluate proposed numbers.

## 4. Status and next execution

**Recommended design direction:** **Reuse and adapt the MO apple tree/fruit as waringo** rather than globally disabling apples or adding a duplicated standalone fruit tree. This is a **design proposal with historical grounding**, not a completed or visually/numerically adopted production asset. The currently merged PR #245 Apple Pie/Cider exclusion remains the accepted executable source. No approved textures or game coefficients accompany this report, and no new crop/harvest code changes should be merged until the design conflicts above are resolved.
