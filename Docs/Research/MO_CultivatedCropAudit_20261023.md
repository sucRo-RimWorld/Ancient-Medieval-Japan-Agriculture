## MO lentil closure — source audit and bounded exclusion, 2026-10-24

**Scope / historical decision:** The MO crop `DankPyon_Plant_Lentils` represents `Lens culinaris`, a Near Eastern domesticate, **not** Japanese soybeans, azuki beans or other attested East Asian legumes. Japanese Bean Association notes that lentils are not cultivated in Japan even in contemporary domestic production (https://www.mame.or.jp/syurui/feature/syurui_14.html). Kumamoto University archaeological crop survey records Japanese archaeological bean evidence mainly for soybean/azuki, with later other legumes (https://www.fhss.kumamoto-u.ac.jp/archaeology/kokuzo/kinds/index.html); neither constitutes exhaustive proof that no one ever imported a lentil before 1600. For AMJ's representative mainland *ordinary crop and food* loop there is **no established historical attestation** justifying this plant's cultivation and Western lentil soup; therefore exclude the original crop and named dish supply rather than replace its plant identity with an unrelated Japanese bean species. **Do not assert lentils were categorically unknown/import-impossible throughout Japanese history.** The project-wide author rule explicitly excludes unsuitable cultivated crops and dependent ordinary dishes as one system.

**Original MO 1.6.2.2 actual XML and targeted implementation:** `Patches/MO_CropExclusion_Lentil.xml` contains six conditional operations, matching seven original XML leaf/Def targets: (1) empty `DankPyon_Plant_Lentils/plant/sowTags`, stopping ordinary Ground/Hydroponics sowing; (2) `DankPyon_RawLentils/tradeability=None`; (3–4) disconnect single and bulk `DankPyon_CookMealWinterLentilSoup` `recipeUsers`; (5) `DankPyon_MealWinterLentilSoup/tradeability=None`; (6) remove the two actual `DankPyon_RawLentils` entries in `DankPyon_HideoutLargeShelf_Vegetables` and `DankPyon_HideOutBuildingBase_Cart_PlantRaw` loot randomItems. The other two `DankPyon_CookMealBoiledBeansAndOnion` recipes **were already disabled** by `MO_CropExclusion_OnionCabbageLemon.xml` and are not redundantly modified. Upstream `DankPyon_RawLentils` original MarketValue1.1, Nutrition0.05, 40-day rot and Winter Soup MarketValue15/Nutrition0.9 are retained for old-save references, **not current-gameplay balancing**.

**Former MO CGB overrides and compatibility:** `Compatibility/MedievalOverhaul/Patches/MO_ExistingCropGrowthBalance.xml` still sets the original retained plant `growDays=8` and `sowMinSkill=2`, replacing MO originals growDays3 and sowMinSkill8. **This crop is no longer sowable** in AMJ+MO, so those prior test/balance records describe historical work, not an active productive crop; the values remain on the preserved ThingDef for save/third-party compatibility. No numeric rebalance is implied and other six MO CGB crops remain unchanged. The original lentil Raw ThingDef, Winter Soup Meal ThingDef, both RecipeDefs, original crop and meal Thought/Hediff references remain present. The patch does not remove old items, old generated containers, saved bills, direct scenario spawns or external Mod explicit StockGenerators. ## MO mushrooms: gather wild, do not farm — 2026-10-24

**Historical distinction:** mushrooms gathered and eaten from wild forests fit Japanese ancient/medieval life. Tokyo University of Agriculture describes ancient Japanese consumption but places the beginning of managed artificial cultivation in the early Edo period (https://www.nodai.ac.jp/research/teacher-column/0301/). MAFF describes the development of artificial cultivation beginning around 300 years ago, particularly shiitake (https://www.maff.go.jp/j/pr/aff/1610/spe1_03.html). These statements do not prohibit natural cave/forest fungi or mushroom-based cooking; they disfavor MO's unrestricted farm-zone mushroom production in dark rooms/hydroponics.

**Actual supply and recipe chain:** In MO 1.6.2.2, `DankPyon_Plant_Mushrooms` has one `plant/sowTags` node (Ground, Hydroponics) and harvests `RawFungus`; the single/bulk `DankPyon_CookMealMushroomSoup` recipes each require the same `RawFungus`. Contrary to the earlier audit's concern, this resource **belongs to Core RimWorld** and can also be obtained by harvesting naturally spawned Agarilux/Glowstool; the planted Nutrifungus source is Ideology DLC, **not the only fungus source**. https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Raw_fungus documents both natural sources and non-Ideology availability. Original MO 1.6.2.2 XML itself does not explicitly spawn its *own* named mushroom plant in the audited paths, so no assertion of natural MO-specific spawn is made. Availability depends on native cave flora and map/environment, not a universally guaranteed stock.

**Implemented limited patch:** `Patches/MO_MushroomCultivationExclusion.xml` uses a single optional-MO `PatchOperationReplace` to empty only `DankPyon_Plant_Mushrooms/plant/sowTags`. Do not change `RawFungus` tradeability or any shared wild plant; retain the original MO mushroom PlantDef, generic cave-spawn behavior where present, all soup ThingDefs/RecipeDefs, all wild-fungus source Defs, original farm stats and cooking work values. **No dedicated artificial-log shiitake grower or new foraging system** is added. The sole operation matches one actual MO 1.6.2.2 target. With no MO, no change. Actual 1.6 no-Ideology final loaded cave fungus, recipe cooking, map variants, preexisting mushroom plants and 1.7 remain **native UNVERIFIED**.

## Grapes, mulberry, and MO fruit-wine supply — 2026-10-24

**Historical split, adopted for bounded MO-source exclusion:** Domestic grape cultivation and edible fruit can be supported from the Kamakura period onward, though the 1186 Koshu discovery date is a traditional story, **not a proven first date**. Japan's first reliable domestic Western-style grape-wine production is Meiji; in late Muromachi/Sengoku foreign wine was imported and drunk by selected elites, and is not therefore categorically excluded as an item. Mulberry trees and silkworm/silk production are ancient Japanese agriculture, already documented by the 3rd century. Ordinary local Western-style mulberry-wine production and circulation are not independently established for medieval Japan. Neither agricultural plant needs to disappear just because MO wires its berries to European barrels.

Historical evidence:
- National Tax Agency, GI Osaka Wine historical brochure: https://www.nta.go.jp/about/organization/osaka/sake/GIOsaka_p/pdf/07220201.pdf (ancient/medieval grapes, early domestic wine attempts)
- Japan Wine official: https://jwine.net/knowledge/advanced-01/ (late medieval imported wine vs no verified pre-Meiji domestic wine)
- Agency for Cultural Affairs, Koshu historic vine equipment: https://kunishitei.bunka.go.jp/heritage/detail/311/00000248 (grape cultivation vs historic winemaking)
- Saitama Prefecture, sericulture timeline: https://www.pref.saitama.lg.jp/a0904/909-20091214-442.html (mulberry/silk attested well before Edo)
- National Diet Library, Japanese sericulture history: https://jpsearch.go.jp/gallery/ndl-PkaAaPDloqW
